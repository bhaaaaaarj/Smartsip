About Smart Sip

Smart Sip is a student science-project prototype designed to help people learn about drink acidity (pH) and carbonation (CO₂).

🔬
Current prototypeBrowse sample drinks and view educational information.
📡
Future sensor connectionA calibrated pH sensor and suitable CO₂ measurement setup can be connected through an ESP32.
📱
QR experienceA QR code can open the published website directly on a phone.
Smart Sip is an educational tool, not a medical or dental diagnostic device. pH alone does not determine the overall health effects of a drink.
